# Opening sequence (Dave, 2026-10-28)

The tutorial puts the player on ANOTHER ship, joined provisionally. That crew is hunting a rogue captain flagged for removal because he or she is reactivating AI too aggressively. Even among thieves there are rules, and watching each other's responsible use of AI is chief among them.

The tutorial introduces the game modes. During the hunt, the host ship is damaged and its captain killed. The player transfers over and becomes captain of the ship that carries the named crew (Abara, Dace, Castellan, Quill, the quartermaster) and Pell.

## Working reading (confirm with Dave)
The ship the player ends up commanding is the rogue captain's ship, where Pell and the named crew already are.

## Design consequences
- The hunt is the tutorial, so it teaches hunt mode before the ship layer exists. The host ship needs only a minimal interior (bridge, maybe one corridor) built from base rooms with different dressing.
- Tight/loose tone starts neutral. Crew relationships start at "stranger who showed up with the people who hunted us".
- The crew served the rogue captain, so their feelings about the player (hunter turned captain) are the starting narrative.
- Pell is the reason the rogue captain was flagged, which ties the AI integration meter to the opening.
- Open: what happened to the rogue captain, who killed the host captain, and how the crew learn Pell's history.
