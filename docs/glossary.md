# Glossary: our terms for the operating rules

Decided Oct 22: invent our own terms, keep the mechanics true to the Star Wolf rundowns (docs/star_wolf_rundowns.md), so there is one set of operating rules. Also decided: the salvage kit system (set bonuses, friction, parasitic heat) stays in scope because it is the ship class system.

| Our term | What it is | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| Afterlight | Old sensor picture (light-lag) | Everything on the display is old by its age |
| The Fan | Where a target could be now | Widens every minute of afterlight; wider if the target is aware |
| Cold running | Silent running | Radiators in, heat goes into the sump |
| Sump | Internal heat sink | Fills while cold running; capacity is a part stat |
| Bloom | Venting or burning flare | Opening radiators or igniting the drive ends cold running |
| Hush | Emission control | No broadcast; any signal is a beacon |
| Snap | Encrypted burst transmission | Spends the burst budget; small signature |
| Pencil beam | Tight-beam laser | Hard to intercept off the beam; needs a line to the receiver |
| Sleeper | Super-chilled missile | Coasts cold, blooms only on final approach; chilling costs heat and time |
| Volley | Group of missiles | Spread or stacked inside the Fan |
| Lure | Decoy or ghost | Splits an incoming volley by fidelity |
| Scatter grid | Kinetic point defense | Fixed channels per window; radiates when active |
| Quench field | Stasis field | Absorbs up to a limit, then collapses |
| Bulkhead drill | Compartmentalization | Limits crew loss when the hull is hit |
| Breach drive | FTL (Hyperstate) drive | Plot, spool, breach; guaranteed escape but loud |
| Knell | Breach shockwave | Heard by every sensor in the system; repeated departure vectors raise relay exposure |
| Common track | Standard departure vector | Fly it for a while before breaching to hide the haven |
| Haven | Hidden dock or relay | Relay exposure stages: quiet, noticed, watched, found |
| Coast / burn | Momentum versus drive ignition | Coasting is free and committed; a burn needs hull rotation and blooms |

## Ship classes (emerge from installed kits)
- Needle: scout parts (mostly Orrin); quiet, quick to spool and turn, weak punch; wins ambushes.
- Hammer: bastion parts (mostly Vask); heavy output and big volleys, loud, slow to turn; wins brawls.
- Anchor: station-led parts (mostly Halden); huge sumps, poor drives; wins long stakeouts.
- Hauler: freighter parts; cheap, forgiving, middling.
- Patchwork: mixed salvage; a gamble that pays off when the fit rolls well.

## Pell integration (Dave, 2026-10-24)
Integration is a campaign-level meter, like a good/evil or trusted/untrusted track in other games, with the same kind of consequences. Higher integration with Pell lowers crew morale and changes trust in conversations with other characters. High and low ends can foreclose or open narrative choices later. It carries across hunts. Not a within-hunt cost.

## After-action and crew report voice (Dave, 2026-10-24)
Crew damage reports are professional and clinical: no damage for a miss, light hull damage for a light hit, possible systems damage for a moderate hit, hull destroyed for a kill.
