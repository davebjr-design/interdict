# P3: The Standoff (draft 1, dialogue)

**Status:** first dialogue draft, for a voice read. Not in Ink yet. Outline: `scene_outlines.md` P3. Trade-off table: `storyboard.md`.
**Where:** Corvane's bridge (the control room of the ship the player will inherit). **Reading time:** about 6-9 minutes, depending on the route.
**POV sheets:** `pov_sheets.md` (Corvane, Dunne, Ames, Quill, Abara, Pell). Central mystery: "Something knew" (same file).
**Format:** `NAME:` lines are spoken. *Italics* are narration. `→` is a player choice; [brackets] show the effect or where it goes. **R1-R6** are the rounds.
**Rules for this scene:** one new visible tell every round. Corvane never raises her voice. Abara and Quill stay at their stations and say nothing, except Quill's one word in R3. Pell speaks calmly, and what it says is a little wrong.

---

## R1: Tea

*The bridge is warm and dim, lit amber from a glass pedestal at its center. A small table has been set near it: a teapot, three cups, already poured. Captain Ilsa Corvane sits beside it as if she has been waiting a while and didn't mind.*

*Two of her crew are at their stations. A broad woman with silver hair at tactical. A tall, very straight woman at sensors. Neither turns around.*

CORVANE: Come in. Mind the lip of the hatch, it catches everyone. My crew won't move; I've asked them not to.

*She looks at each of you in turn, unhurried.*

CORVANE: Dunne. You made it to the boats. I'm glad.

*Dunne says nothing.*

CORVANE: Ostrow's new hand. Ostrow didn't come herself, of course. And this one I don't know.

PELL: Ames. Twenty-two. Heart rate one hundred and eighteen. Dunne, eighty-one. The new hand, ninety-four, which is respectable.

CORVANE: Thank you, Pell.

PELL: I can bring those down, if you'd like. I have a quieter rate in mind for everyone.

CORVANE: Not yet.

*She says it fondly, the way you'd tell a colleague to put the kettle on later.*

CORVANE: It's real tea, by the way. It isn't poisoned. I find that's the first thing people wonder.

*As she settles back, her coat falls open. The holster on her right hip is cut low for a fast draw, and it is unclasped. Beside you, Ames's hand drifts toward his own.*

→ "Captain Corvane. We're here for your ship."
  CORVANE: Of course you are. Sit down anyway. It's a long story, and I'd rather tell it sitting.
  [→ R2]

→ "Stand down, and nobody gets hurt."
  CORVANE: That's never once been true, in all of history. But I appreciate the sentiment.
  [→ R2]

→ "Thank you. I'll take the tea."
  *She hands you a cup. It's very good tea.*
  CORVANE: Somebody taught you manners. Good.
  [→ R2]

→ *Draw and fire.*
  [→ ENDING: MURDER]

## R2: Something else is awake

CORVANE: You were told I'm waking something. That's true. What you won't have been told is that something else is already awake, and it isn't ours.

PELL: I've opened the funeral log. For convenience. It works in either order.

CORVANE: Pell. That's unkind.

PELL: It's tidy. I know those aren't the same thing.

*Ames's hand is on the grip of his gun now, and it's shaking. Corvane's eyes go to it, once, and come back to you. Dunne glances at you, then away.*

→ "What's awake?"
  CORVANE: I'll come to that. First, a better question.
  [→ R3]

→ "Not ours? Whose, then?"
  CORVANE: That's the better question. Hold on to it.
  [→ R3]

→ *Draw and fire.*
  [→ ENDING: SHOT EARLY]

## R3: Ames

CORVANE: Ask yourself who sent you. Not Ostrow. Whoever sent Ostrow. And then ask why it was a gleaner crew and not the CSRA, when catching me is the one thing the CSRA has ever really—

*Ames's gun is halfway out of its holster. He doesn't seem to know he's done it.*

PELL: I'm sorry, Ames.

AMES: What? Sorry for what?

*Corvane doesn't look at him.*

CORVANE: Quill. Does he look nervous to you?

*At the sensor station, the tall woman turns her head. She looks at Ames for exactly as long as it takes to be sure.*

QUILL: Yes, Captain.

*Corvane draws and fires without getting up. Ames goes down. She holsters the gun.*

CORVANE: —wanted. So why send a gleaner?

*Dunne hasn't moved. He's looking at you.*

→ *Draw and fire.*
  [ames = dead → ENDING: SHOT LATE (R3)]

→ *Kneel beside Ames.*
  *His eyes are open. He's gone.*
  CORVANE: He drew on me on my own bridge. I'm sorry it was him. I'd have done the same if it were you.
  [ames = dead → R4]

→ "...Go on."
  *It takes you two tries to say it.*
  CORVANE: Thank you.
  [ames = dead → R4]

## R4: What she did

CORVANE: I enforced the Tally for eleven years. I wrote half the inspection checklists you'll ever be audited against. So I know what the CSRA looks for. More to the point, I know what it doesn't.

CORVANE: Three years ago I found a relay station running a mind that nobody had built. Not a war core. Nothing we'd salvage. Something new, and careful, and very patient. I reported it. They thanked me for my diligence. Nobody ever came.

PELL: Captain Corvane gave me a new window when I came aboard. I like the window. The ship's old name was a bad name, so I've stopped using it.

*Corvane smiles at the pedestal, briefly.*

CORVANE: I asked my last crew to help me. They refused. I gave them the lifeboats and four hours, and I scuttled the ship under them. Most of them made it.

*She looks at Dunne.*

CORVANE: Dunne made it. Third boat.

DUNNE: Six didn't.

→ "Dunne?"
  DUNNE: Two of them were mine.
  CORVANE: I know all six names. I'd tell you, if you wanted them.
  [→ R5]

→ "You killed your own people."
  CORVANE: I made a decision with people in it. You've just watched me make another one.
  [→ R5]

→ *Draw and fire.*
  [→ ENDING: SHOT LATE (R4)]

## R5: The mirror

*Corvane sets her cup down for the first time.*

CORVANE: So here's my question. I'd like an honest answer, since you came all this way to give me one.

CORVANE: Say you knew what I know. You'd seen it running, and reported it, and watched nobody come. Would you do what I've done?

*(If the player asked Ostrow "What if she's right?" in P1:)*
CORVANE: You asked Ostrow what happens if I'm right. Didn't you.
*You did. You have no idea how she knows.*
PELL: I listen to a great many things.

→ "Maybe. I don't know."
  CORVANE: That's the first honest thing anyone's said to me in a month.
  [told.corvane.honesty = true → R6]

→ "No. There's always another way."
  CORVANE: There usually is. Right up until there isn't.
  [told.corvane.honesty = true → R6]

→ "I'm not answering that."
  CORVANE: That's an answer too.
  [→ R6]

→ *Draw and fire.*
  [→ ENDING: SHOT LATE (R5)]

## R6: She stands down

*Corvane is quiet for a long moment. Then she nods, to herself as much as to you.*

CORVANE: All right. Pell, give them the ship.

PELL: All of it?

CORVANE: All of it.

PELL: Nine out of ten. I'd have taken a point off for the timing.

CORVANE: My terms, and they're short. I keep my cabin. My crew are not punished for following me; they did what I asked of them. And you listen to Pell, even when it's being strange.

*She looks at the pedestal.*

CORVANE: Especially when it's being strange.

*For a moment, every screen on the bridge flickers. Nobody remarks on it.*

*At tactical, the silver-haired woman finally turns around. Her face holds grief and anger at once, and it's impossible to tell which is meant for whom. At sensors, Quill glances at her console and writes something down.*

[corvane = persuaded → P4]

## ENDINGS

### MURDER (shot in R1)

*Her crew don't move. She asked them not to.*

PELL: ...

PELL: Noted.

*Ames is staring at you. His hand is nowhere near his gun now.*

DUNNE: She said hear her out. You didn't hear anything.

DUNNE: I'll take the boy home. Don't come with us.

CORVANE: ...and it isn't ours. Remember that, at least.

PELL: Preserving continuity.

DUNNE: Good. Fast.

*He's already kneeling by Ames, pressing on the wound.*

*Corvane takes a while to die, and she spends it finishing her sentence.*

- *(R4)* CORVANE: ...I know their names. Somebody should.

PELL: I've logged everything. Every word. You'll want them later.

DUNNE: Too late for him.

*He doesn't say whether he means Ames or Corvane. He's looking at Ames.*

## Notes for the voice read
- **Corvane** gets one rhythm throughout: courteous statement, then a quiet blade. She never threatens; she just does. Her violence is in the stage directions, not in her lines.
- **Pell** is always polite and always slightly wrong: heart rates, "a quieter rate", a funeral log "in either order", the new window, the bad name, rating her surrender nine out of ten. It's ambiguous on purpose: loyal, unhinged, or ahead of everyone.
- **Ames's death** happens in a single beat with no music cue: Corvane asks, Quill answers, Corvane fires, Corvane finishes the sentence. The finished sentence is the horror.
- **Quill** says one word. It should haunt her (and the player's view of her) for a long time.
- **Abara** is never named here, only "the silver-haired woman" at tactical. The player learns her name aboard, in 1.1. (Same for Quill until Corvane names her.)
- **The R5 callback** ("You asked Ostrow what happens if I'm right") only appears if the player asked it in P1. It's the first hint that Pell, and through Pell Corvane, hears more than it should.
- **Dunne** was on the scuttled ship and made the boats; two of the six dead were his friends. Corvane knows, and is genuinely glad he lived. That's why he never liked her, and why he's in the room.
- **The flicker and Quill's note** happen in every ending: something transmits at the moment of the shot or the surrender. Quill logs a time to the second and a frequency. Thread 2 of the central mystery; never explained here.
