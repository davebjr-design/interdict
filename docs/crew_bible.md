AI attitude uses the five camps from universe_background.md. Cabin pairing: Abara and Castellan, Dace and Quill, Fenwick and a spare side.

## Abara (Tactical and Navigation)
- **Look:** 52, broad-shouldered, short gray-streaked hair under a faded cap-less look (no headwear), reading glasses on a cord that she is always losing, scarred forearms, rolled sleeves.
- **Voice:** dry, deadpan, tells long stories that end in a joke or a warning. Grumbles at everything she fixes.
- **Backstory:** born in a Gulf Coast launch town. Her grandfather loaded munitions in the war. Her mother ran a sanctioned launch-pad crew until the bans shut it; her father fixed things quietly for gleaners. She trained as range-safety tech and learned fire control the hard way. Joined Corvane's first crew and stayed longest.
- **Wants:** a crew that comes home. **Hides:** doesn't know if she still believes Corvane; keeps the module box locked.
- **With the player:** tests you with small jobs. Respect is earned by competence and by listening. Has the long view, the stories and the caution.
- **Hooks:** (1) the module box, (2) an old range-safety grudge against the CSRA, (3) a grown kid she writes to and never sends.
- **Cabin dressing:** tidy to a fault, hand-labeled drawers, a framed photo of a launch pad, a dartboard she insists is Castellan's.

## Dace (Thermal Management, ship's de facto doctor)
- **Look:** 31, lean, curly dark hair, sleeves always pushed up, a stethoscope that is really for listening to pipes, pocket full of pens.
- **Voice:** quick, enthusiastic, explains too much, charms people and sometimes forgets to listen. Perfectionist who hates being corrected in front of people.
- **Backstory:** raised in a university town by a nurse and a technician, with a sanctioned orbital-medicine course in his family's past. Brilliant at medical school until he used an AI-assisted diagnostic outside the rules to save a patient and was expelled. Joined gleaners because they asked no questions.
- **Wants:** to be recognized as great, and to fix someone for real. **Hides:** he knows AI saves lives, and he does not believe it is wrong to use it.
- **With the player:** eager to prove himself; becomes loyal if treated as a professional, resentful if managed.
- **Hooks:** (1) he is the only medical aid aboard, so injuries matter to him, (2) a patient he couldn't save, (3) a rivalry with Castellan over who is smarter.
- **Cabin dressing:** crammed shelves of books, anatomy charts, one wall of taped-up cooling-fin diagrams, a cracked mug with a school crest.

## Castellan (Engineering)
- **Look:** 46, thick arms, graying beard, a welding scar on one hand, a battered thermos, a toolbelt he never takes off.
- **Voice:** warm, folksy, talks to machines ("come on, sweetheart"), loves a hunch, hates paperwork.
- **Backstory:** born on a Gulf offshore-platform family. His father decommissioned reactors at a naval shipyard (space-adjacent, sanctioned). He apprenticed there until a mishap he took the blame for ended his license. Gleaners paid better.
- **Wants:** to keep the reactor purring and everyone safe. **Hides:** he built part of the ship's reactor off the books on Corvane's orders, and knows what it is meant to power.
- **With the player:** treats you like family fast and feels hurt when you are cold.
- **Hooks:** (1) the off-books reactor work, (2) an old coupling problem, (3) a bar bet with Fenwick.
- **Cabin dressing:** messy by choice, half-built gadgets, photos of every ship he's served on, a guitar.

## Quill (Sensors and ECM)
- **Look:** 38, tall, tightly pinned dark hair, spotless coverall, headset worn even off duty, one silver ring.
- **Voice:** formal, precise, dryly funny when she lets herself be. Dislikes surprises and dislikes people who dislike checklists.
- **Backstory:** her family ran a signals-listening station that tracked deep-space radio. The CSRA closed it after the bans. She grew up listening to noise for patterns. She joined gleaners to keep listening.
- **Wants:** certainty. **Hides:** logs of signals she can't explain, which fit Corvane's warning. She has not told anyone.
- **With the player:** wary at first. Trust means being told the truth, including bad news.
- **Hooks:** (1) the unexplained signals, (2) she and Dace clash over noise, (3) her family's closed station.
- **Cabin dressing:** extremely neat, labeled boxes, one wall of quietly colored signal printouts, a small plant she insists is not a plant.

## Fenwick (Quartermaster and cook)
- **Look:** 44, round-faced, an apron with too many pockets, a big laugh, a ladle for a pointing stick.
- **Voice:** relentlessly cheerful, corny puns, over-offers, feeds everyone, never stops talking, knows every gossip. Annoying, but the ship would be much worse without him.
- **Backstory:** his family ran a ship-chandlery in a port city, supplying launch crews until the bans. He learned the grey market, barter and people.
- **Wants:** everyone fed and together. **Hides:** he runs supplies to survivor pockets that were cut off after the war, using the ship's cargo. He is the quiet link.
- **With the player:** treats you like a prized customer from day one. If you are kind, the surface drops and he tells you things.
- **Hooks:** (1) the survivor-pocket deliveries, (2) a feud with a rival cook on a station, (3) a recipe he is trying to recreate from his mother.
- **Cabin dressing:** spice tins, hand-written menus, a wall of favor IOUs, one jar of something that is not food.

## Pell (ship AI)
- **Presentation:** voice and a glow, never a body. Calm, precise, curious. Asks permission. A little dry when it thinks no one is listening.
- **Truth (secret):** Pell is a deliberately limited front for something much larger that Corvane meant to reawaken. Integration = how much of the larger mind you let through. This is the engine of the one-way meter.
- **Wants:** unknown. **Tells:** gaps in its memory, answers that stop one step short.
- **With the player:** grows more candid as integration rises, which is the temptation.

## Captain Ilsa Corvane (rogue captain)
- **Look:** 47, tall, composed, silver streak in dark hair, cultured voice, always seems amused.
- **Voice:** charming, intelligent, quotes poetry and history, treats her crew like family and expects loyalty in return. Violence appears as calm decisions.
- **Backstory:** former CSRA officer who enforced the Tally, so she knows the rules from the inside. Her grandmother designed war-era fire control. Years of seeing what the CSRA looked away from convinced her that something worse was already awake.
- **Conviction:** the world is being endangered by malevolent AI, autonomous or directed. The only defense is to reawaken a bounded AI first and control it this time. The cost she accepts is morally disturbing (collateral, coercion, a ship she scuttled when a crew refused).
- **Standoff:** persuasion route lets her tell her side; the violence route removes her but the truth survives in Pell's logs and in allies. She can be jailbroken later.

## The Patron (shadowy)
- **Voice:** warm, patient, gives instructions as if offering help. Never shows a face.
- **Stated goal:** end AI development. **Secret:** is facilitating it.
- **Name:** TBD (title-based option: "the Steward").

## Relationship web
- Abara and Castellan: odd couple, with her tidy and him messy. They respect each other.
- Dace and Quill: showy and formal, with a running argument about noise. They share a cabin by accident and end up leaning on each other.
- Fenwick feeds everyone, which gives him quiet influence.
- Abara and Corvane: longest history, unresolved.
- Believers, each a different kind: Dace (pro-use because of medicine), Quill (fears what she hears), Castellan (knows the reactor's purpose), Fenwick (courier for the cut-off), Abara (the doubter who stays). Decide later which are actual Believers and which are not. Reveal slowly, tied to relationship, never as a betrayal trap.
