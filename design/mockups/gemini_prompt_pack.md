# Gemini prompt pack: CIC concept art

Attach cic_room_free.png (layout reference) to every prompt. Extra attachments per prompt are noted.
Gemini images are for look and mood only; game screens stay rendered from the game itself.

## Style block (paste at the top of every prompt)
Concept art for a hard-sci-fi space-submarine combat information centre, in the analogue, lived-in style of Battlestar Galactica (2004) CIC and a Cold War submarine control room. Rounded, curved console banks, riveted painted steel, olive-grey and muted teal paint with worn edges, stencilled labels, chunky toggle switches and guarded buttons, cable looms overhead, corded wired handsets, grease-pencil marks on plexiglass, paper plot sheets and clipboards. Screens are small CRT-style phosphor displays, not sleek touchscreens. Low warm tungsten pools of light against dark teal shadow, light haze. Practical, tired, human, no glossy futurism, no neon holograms except one dim table projection. Keep the exact room layout and camera from the attached image: circular room, central round map table, three curved console banks, three reclining crash couches.

## 1. Room, free  (attach: cic_room_free.png)
Repaint the attached image in the style above. Keep layout and camera. Show a crew of three at their couches, harnesses hanging open, a coffee cup clipped to a bench, a captain standing at the table.

## 2. Room, braced  (attach: cic_room_free.png, cic_room_brace.png)
Same room, same camera, during a hard burn. Everyone reclined and strapped into 5-point harnesses, couches gimballed back, loose objects tethered, red warning lamps washing the room, one amber BRACE annunciator lit.

## 3. Section view  (attach: cic_room_free.png, cic_sensors.png)
Close on the sensors bank from the attached image. Three curved CRT screens, a grab rail, a bank of toggle switches and a wired handset, an officer reclined in the couch with harness straps. Keep the composition.

## 4. Table  (attach: cic_room_free.png, cic_table.png)
Close on the central round map table: a dark rimmed table with a dim projected 3D sphere, a few coloured contact markers (yellow, red, blue, teal), brass rim, paper chart clipped nearby. Keep it analogue, no sci-fi glow beyond the projection.

## 5. Couch sheet (optional, for Meshy later)
