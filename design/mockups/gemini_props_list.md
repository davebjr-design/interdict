# Gemini props list: CIC and ship props (Interdict)

Scope: everything physical except character/environment animation and the screen displays (those stay game-rendered).
Look target: the approved Gemini concept sheet (inked, flat-coloured, analogue, dark teal-grey steel, warm brass, amber/red only for alerts, round shapes).

## How to use
1. Paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then one prop prompt. Generate each prop as ONE image: a model sheet on a plain flat mid-grey background, no scene, no shadows on the ground, even light.
2. Sheet layout for every prop: front, side, back, three-quarter, plus one detail inset. Orthographic, same scale, labelled in small stencil text.
3. For small props generate several per image ("prop family sheets") where marked SET.
4. Ask for: no text except stencil labels, no logos, no people, no hands.

## STYLE BLOCK (paste at top of every prompt)
Prop model sheet in the style of an inked graphic-novel concept illustration: flat colour with bold dark outlines and light cross-hatching, no glossy rendering. Analogue, lived-in, utilitarian hard-sci-fi submarine/spacecraft hardware, in the spirit of the Battlestar Galactica (2004) CIC and Cold War submarines. Palette: dark teal-grey and olive painted steel, worn edges showing bare metal, warm brass and muted yellow for rails and trim, amber and red only for warnings. Rounded corners, riveted panels, stencilled labels, coiled cables, chunky switches. Plain flat mid-grey background, orthographic views (front, side, back, three-quarter) plus one detail inset, labelled.

## A. SEATING AND SECURING (highest priority: brace mechanic)

A1. Crash couch (officer). A heavy reclining acceleration couch for a crew member. Round pedestal base with a gimbal ring and locking collar; seat pan with thick padding; reclining back with a headrest; leg rest that raises; two padded armrests with a flat keypad panel on each; fold-out footplate. Coiled cable from the pedestal. Worn olive-drab upholstery, stitched seams. Show it upright (sitting) and fully reclined (brace) in the same sheet.

A2. Crash couch (commanding officer). Same family as A1 but a little larger and more worn, with a brass nameplate on the headrest, a mounting for a call-deck key pad on each armrest (8 chunky keys: 1-4, Enter, Space, Backspace, R), and a small handset holster on the left arm.

A3. Five-point harness. Shoulder straps, lap belt, crotch strap, central round quick-release buckle with a rotary latch, adjusters, orange-red webbing with stencilled markings. Show it buckled on an empty couch and open and hanging.

A4. Jump seat (folding). A fold-down utility seat on a wall bracket for an extra crew member, thin padding, a lap belt, a spring latch. Show stowed and deployed.

A5. Seat gimbal mechanism detail. A cutaway of the pedestal: heavy ring bearing, hydraulic dampers, locking pins, a cable spool. Used for close-ups and texture reference.

A6. Grab rails and handholds (SET). Brass-yellow tubular rail sections: straight, curved arc to follow a console, corner brackets, ceiling-mounted overhead rail, recessed pull-handle, a loop strap. Show mounting hardware.

A7. Foot loops and magnetic boot plates (SET). Floor-mounted foot loops, a hinged boot cradle, a magnetic plate with worn yellow hazard stripes.

A8. Tether and carabiner set (SET). Coiled tethers with spring clips for tools, clipboards and mugs; labelled hooks.

## B. CONSOLES AND CONTROLS

B1. Console bench, curved (three variants SET). A curved bench with a sloped front, slanted top panel, stencilled labels, a rolled brass edge rail and a recessed knee space. Variants: three-bay (sensors), two-bay (attack, taller), three-bay low (systems). Panel-seam lines, a few rivets, cable ducts at the base. No screens, leave the screen openings as empty bezel frames.

B2. CRT monitor housing (SET). Chunky rounded bezel around a small curved-glass screen, in three sizes (small, medium, wide). Side bezel carries a column of backlit function buttons, a brightness knob, a stencilled label plate. Screen area left plain dark so we can overlay game graphics.

B3. Switch bank (SET). Toggle switches (chunky, with guard covers), rotary selector knobs with pointer, rocker switches, push-to-test lamps in amber, red and white, a small key switch. Mounted on painted panels with stencil labels. Show four panel layouts.

B4. Guarded FIRE button assembly. A red mushroom button under a hinged, transparent yellow-and-black hazard-striped guard cover, a safety wire seal, and a pair of small status lamps. Show closed and lifted.

B5. Autonomy rotary selectors (SET). Three-position rotary selectors (Auto / Supervised / Hands-on) with chunky pointer knobs and engraved dials. Brass and black.

B6. Annunciator panel (hardware). A horizontal overhead panel housing a grid of rectangular backlit legend windows, six groups of eight, with a master WARNING and master CAUTION lamp and an ACK button on the left. Show lit amber, red and white examples.

B7. Dials and gauges (SET). Round analogue gauges (heat sink level, hull pressure, power), a bar-graph column, a pair of VU-style meters, a clock with a stopwatch hand, a compass-style bearing repeater. Brass bezels, glass reflections, cracked glass on one.

B8. Keypads and plotting tools (SET). A numeric keypad, a trackball with brass collar, a joystick with a trigger, a rotary encoder dial, a light-pen on a coiled cord.

B9. Systems rack. A floor-to-ceiling equipment rack: stacked rack modules with vents, cooling fans, labelled patch panels, blinking indicator lamps, tied cable looms. Door open and closed.

B10. Wall-mounted breaker panel. Hinged cover, rows of breakers, a stencilled warning plate, a red emergency handle.

## C. THE MAP TABLE

C1. Map table pedestal and rim. A round table, dark painted steel, tapered pedestal base with maintenance panels, a flat dark central disc, a polished brass rim ring with six small inset knobs and grab-loops, a recessed emitter lens at the centre. Show top, side and underside.

C2. Table projection emitter. A recessed brass-ringed lens array in the centre of the table, with focusing rings and a small cable gland. (The hologram itself is rendered by the game.)

C3. Plot sheets and clipboards (SET). Paper plot sheets with grid and range rings drawn on, grease-pencil bearing lines, torn corners, tape; clipboards with pencil on a string; a folded fleet-orders map. Leave the content simple and generic.

C4. Grease-pencil and plexiglass overlay. A transparent plexiglass overlay for the table with grease-pencil annotations (bearing lines, range arcs, contact marks), hinged on one side with a thumb latch.

C5. Table accessories (SET). A parallel ruler, brass dividers, a protractor, a stopwatch, a stack of index cards, a magnetic marker tray.

## D. COMMS AND HANDHELDS

D1. Wired handset. Heavy bakelite-style telephone handset with a coiled cord, a push-to-talk bar, a hook cradle on the console edge. Show on the hook and off.

D2. Intercom box. A wall-mounted intercom with a speaker grille, a row of push-buttons (SENS, FIRE, WEAP, ENG, DMG), a press-to-talk lever, a small amber lamp.

D3. Headsets (SET). A cloth-padded headset with a boom mic, a lightweight earpiece, and a coiled cable with a quick-disconnect.

D4. PA speaker and ship's call horn. A round wall-mounted loudspeaker and a brass call horn with a small bell on a bracket.

D5. Datapad / slate. A rugged, thick, slightly rounded tablet with a physical button strip, a hand strap, a scratched screen area left dark.

## E. CREW PERSONAL AND ROOM CLUTTER (set dressing)

E1. Mugs and drinks (SET). A steel mug with a tether clip, a squeeze bulb with a straw, a flask, a plastic cup clipped to a bench.

E2. Paperwork (SET). A stack of printouts, loose forms, a logbook with a ribbon, a pencil, a paper tray, a hole-punched binder.

E3. Tools (SET). A multi-tool, a torch, a screwdriver set, a small wrench, a pair of pliers, all with lanyard loops.

E4. Personal items (SET). A pinned photo, a small mascot, a worn playing-card deck, a St-Christopher-style charm, a cigarette-pack-sized tin. Generic, nothing branded.

E5. Rubbish and wear (SET). A crumpled paper cup, a spent sticky note, a dented ration tin, scuff-decal sheets for panels.

## F. ROOM FIXTURES

F1. Wall panel segments (SET). Curved riveted steel wall panels with a seam pattern, access hatches, painted pipe runs, a conduit with brass clips, painted pipe numbers.

F2. Overhead cable looms (SET). Thick bundles of black cable with white lacing and labels, hanging in swags, with brass clamps and a drip tray.

F3. Light fittings (SET). A round recessed ceiling lamp with a wire guard, a pool-light spot, a red emergency strobe, a small amber work-light, and a green exit marker.

F4. Hatch and door. A round pressure hatch with a spoked wheel, rubber seal, hazard stripes, a stencilled label, a coaming ring in the floor. Show open and closed.

F5. Floor tiles and deck markings. A set of floor tile types: non-slip hexagon tread, hazard-stripe edging, white range rings, painted station boxes. (Seamless texture sheet.)

F6. Stowage lockers and racks (SET). A wall locker with a latch, a rack of emergency masks, a fire extinguisher bracket, a first-aid cabinet, a hand-held fire axe, a tool board with painted outlines.

F7. Signs and stencils (SET). Painted stencil labels: STATION names, EMERGENCY, BRACE, STOW, NO LOOSE ITEMS, directional arrows, valve tags. Generic fictional text only.

## G. SHIP HARDWARE THAT APPEARS IN GAMEPLAY

G1. Missile / salvo cartridge. A compact missile, nose cone, small fins, a seeker window, a colour-coded stripe for the warhead type (HE red, EMP blue, flare yellow). Show one and a six-pack salvo block.

G2. Magazine rack. A cylindrical magazine drum with 6-round clips, a hoist, and a stencilled counter; show 10 clips.

G3. Probe set carriers (SET). Three makers, each a different design: Orrin (slim, quiet, polished, fragile), Vask (rugged, thick, loud, exposed radio mast), Halden (mixed, repairable, panels with screws). Each set: a rack with 2-6 probes, a latch cradle, a maker plate.

G4. Single probe (SET). One probe per maker, front/side/back: sensor aperture, laser emitter, radio mast, stowed fins. Small, about the size of a boot.

G5. Countermeasure canisters (SET). Flare canister, decoy pod, and a launch tube with a breech.

G6. Fuel and heat sink hardware. A coolant tank with a viewing window, a heat-exchanger fin block, a vent hatch, labelled valves.

G7. Damage-control kit (SET). A patch kit, a breach plug (conical), a clamp, a fire hose coil, an extinguisher.

G8. Wreck and salvage props (SET). A scorched panel, a twisted strut, a torn bulkhead, a half-open crate with salvage inside. (For the salvage scenes.)

## H. TEXTURE SHEETS (flat, seamless where noted)

H1. Panel paint and wear (seamless). Dark teal-grey painted steel with chipped edges, rivet rows, seams, light grime and tidemarks.

H2. Upholstery (seamless). Olive-drab stitched padding, wear at the edges, faded stencil.

H3. Brass and chrome (seamless). Worn brass, scratched chrome, a fingerprint pattern.

H4. Decal sheet. Hazard stripes, arrows, stencils, small warning plates, serial plates, scuffs, dirt streaks, water stains.

H5. Webbing (seamless). Orange-red straps with a diamond stitch pattern.

## NOT covered here
Crew character art and rigs, animation, the CRT/hologram displays, and sound. Screens: leave them blank in all prompts above.
