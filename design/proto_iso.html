Ship log
The control room, forward end of the upper deck. Navigation at the far end, the attack center amidships around the chart table, sensors and ECM aft. Pell's core is the pedestal on the centerline. The hatch aft leads to the junction.

Hull revision 2: compartments chain end to end through bulkhead hatches, with a ladder between decks at the junction. Three of twelve compartments are wired; the rest have art but no walk grid yet, and their hatches say so. Room art is generated from the submarine brief in docs/room_art_brief_v2.md and keyed from magenta. Crew are Meshy models rendered to sprite sheets, drawn at 90% of a hatch height. Light flicker is drawn in code over the still art. Walk grids are fitted by hand; use Show walk grid to check them.
