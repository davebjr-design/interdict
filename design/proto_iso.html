The Tern's control room. Captain Ostrow keeps her ship painted, stowed and quiet. You're the probationary hand, and you're late for the briefing at the chart table.

Hull revision 2: all twelve compartments are walkable. They chain end to end through bulkhead hatches, with ladders between decks at the junction and in engineering. Every room is scaled to the same walk-cell size, so the captain stays the same size throughout and the view only zooms enough to fill the stage. Room art is generated from the submarine brief in docs/room_art_brief_v2.md and keyed from magenta. Crew are Meshy models rendered to sprite sheets, drawn at 90% of a hatch height. Light flicker is drawn in code over the still art. Walk grids are fitted by hand; use Show walk grid to check them.
