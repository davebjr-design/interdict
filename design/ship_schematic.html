Interdict · hull layout, revision 2

Interdict Hull Schematic

Two decks, bow to the right. Compartments chain end to end through bulkhead hatches, the way they do on a boat, so there is no corridor whose length has to match the rooms behind it. Both decks run the same 76 m, which is what keeps the map legible.

Hull
About 100 m overall, 76 m of pressure hull in compartments, roughly 10 m across. Two decks of 2.3 m clear.
Missiles
24 tubes in two rows of 12. Twelve are live: six missiles racked around a handling hub, 72 in all, one tube per salvo. Twelve salvos of six.
Salvage
The other twelve tubes are racked with canisters. That is the cargo hold, and it is why a crew of 28 is flying this much hull.
Coil gun
A 65 m keel accelerator for close range. Breech and slug magazine at the forward end of lower engineering; muzzle at the bow. Fire control sits in the control room.
Heat
Sink loops run the whole hull, dorsal panels and keel runs both. Thermal control is Dace’s compartment on the upper deck, with the sink machinery below it in lower engineering.
Circuit
Aft along the upper deck, down the engineering ladder, forward along the lower deck, up the junction ladder. About 150 m on foot, so the deck plan needs click-to-travel.

What this changes

  • The spine corridor is gone. Compartments connect directly, which is what fixes the spacing problem.
  • Every room needs regenerating as a long, narrow compartment. The current bridge, mess, junction and corridor art do not fit the new proportions. That is about 12 credits each.
  • Engineering is two scenes drawn as one space: the reactor casing holds the same plan position in both, you see its top through the upper grating and its base below, and the ladder lands in the same spot.
  • Two ladders only, at the junction and in engineering. That is enough to make the circuit, and a third would just be a shortcut.
  • Click-to-travel on the deck plan stops being optional at twelve scenes across two decks.

Measurements are drawn to scale at 12 px per meter. Cell counts assume one grid cell is about one meter, which is what the walk grid already uses.
