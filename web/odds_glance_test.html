Odds Screen Glance Test

Show one person this screen for five seconds, hide it, then ask what they saw. Three testers. This is the Week 0 comprehension check for the firing decision.

How to run it

  • Hand the tester the device. Say only: "You are the captain. You have five seconds to read this, then I will ask what you saw."
  • Press Show screen. It hides itself after five seconds. Do not explain anything.
  • Read the three questions aloud (or let them tap), then ask the last one in their own words.
  • Record each tester with Save tester. After three, the verdict appears.

Pass: at least two of three testers get the odds and the waiting question right. Fail on either means the screen needs a redesign before Stage 1.
