Is generated speech viable for the dialogue?

Three tests on one page: (1) pre-rendered neural voices, (2) casting audition, (3) your browser's built-in speech, with a diagnostic so we can see why it fails. Keep this file, voice.js, voice_clips/ and voice_samples/ together; it works when opened directly.

4. What it means for a large amount of dialogue

Browser speechPre-rendered neural (Piper)
CostFree, nothing to shipFree; about 30 KB of audio per 4-second line, so 1,000 lines is about 30 MB
QualityVaries by device and browser; often flat; cannot be tunedConsistent everywhere; clearly better than typical built-in voices; still synthetic
ControlPitch and rate onlyPer-character voice, pace and variation; re-render a single line in about 1-2 seconds
ReliabilityFailed for you; needs a user gesture, voices load late, differs per OSPlain audio files: works anywhere audio works
WorkflowNone neededWrite lines, run one script, only changed lines are re-rendered
Dynamic lines (numbers, bearings, track names)FineNeeds a template (e.g. "bearing" + digit clips) or a browser fallback

Recommendation: write dialogue as data, render with Piper at build time, keep browser speech only as a fallback for lines not yet rendered. A professional voice service or human actors would be a later upgrade for key scenes; the pipeline does not change.
